Whereabouts

A clickable demo of a self-hosted app for finding where things are kept at home, by search, voice or a QR label on the box.

  • Tap the mic on the home screen to ask where something is.
  • Under Changes, undo something your partner moved. Then try Pinned and Lent out.
  • Open Rooms, then Garage, and tap the + to create a box and its label.
  • In a box, try Add by voice and merge the duplicate.
  • Move an item, then undo it from History.
  • Tap the centre + for bulk import from a spreadsheet.
  • Tap + and choose Start a move. Bring your labelled boxes, pack a new one, scan to unpack, then finish the move.
  • Tap the home name at the top to switch homes, including the New house.
  • In Garage › Shelf 1, open the Fishing crate to find a box inside a box.

Sample data, scripted voice and scanning, and decorative QR codes. Changes reset when you reload.
